# Amazon-Business-Insights-actionAnalysis
Amazon e-commerce business analysis dashboard using Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Amazon e-commerce data using Power BI to understand business performance and identify useful insights.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, products, customers, orders, and profitability. The analysis helps understand business performance and identify areas that may require attention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall business and sales performance
* Understand product-wise performance
* Analyze customer behavior
* Track profitability and profit margins
* Identify important business insights from the data
* Create an interactive and easy-to-understand Power BI dashboard

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation
* **DAX** – Creating measures and calculated metrics
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data storage, preparation, and analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides an overall summary of the business using key performance indicators such as:

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders
* Quantity Sold

### 2. Product Performance

Analyzes product-level performance to understand:

* Revenue by product
* Profit by product
* Product sales performance
* Product quantity
* Product performance patterns

### 3. Customer Behaviour

Focuses on customer-related analysis, including:

* Customer types
* New and existing customers
* Prime membership
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Orders and sales patterns

### 4. Profitability Analysis

Provides a detailed view of profitability, including:

* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Profit by product
* Revenue vs. Profit
* Loss and return-related analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

The dashboard was created to identify patterns and trends such as:

* Products contributing significantly to revenue and profit
* Differences in customer purchasing behavior
* The relationship between revenue and profitability
* Impact of returns and cancellations on business performance
* Products and areas that require further business attention
